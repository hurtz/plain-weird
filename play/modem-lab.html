      Good to know
      
      
      
        
      Two lanes or one?
      Up through 2400 bps, each direction got its own slice of the band. From V.32 on, both sides talk on the same frequencies at once and echo cancellers subtract each modem's own voice.
      
        
      Why 56K needed an ISP.
      56K works because the ISP's end is digital. Only one analog to digital conversion can sit between you and them, so two home modems never got past 33.6k.
      
        
      AT means Attention.
      Hayes put every command behind AT in 1981, and nearly every modem after copied it. That is why the commands in the decoder all start the same way.
      
        
      How this works.
      Standard handshakes are generated from the published specs: Bell 103 and 212A, V.21, V.22bis, V.25, V.32bis, V.8, V.34 and V.90. HST, PEP, CSP, V.FC and V.fast were proprietary and are marked as approximations.

        Help
        
    
        
      
        
        
        Pick your modem, pick who you dial, set the line, then dial. You hear the real handshake, built from the published standards, while the page shows who is talking and which standard says so.
        
        
        Click a number on the timeline to hear just that step. Press Mom picks up, Call waiting, Lightning or Hang up properly to see what went wrong in the 90s.
