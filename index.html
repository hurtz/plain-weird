Plain Weird

Games, teardowns, and strange machines by Jason O'Connell

I've been in IT for 32 years, starting at a Phoenix computer store in 1994. Now I build at night with AI: remakes of the games I grew up on, teardowns of their code, a TV I can swear at, a radio scanner that writes the news, and an AI that phones businesses for me. Most of the games below run right here in your browser. Hit play.

Open in its own tab
Made in Des Moines, Iowa. Built with Claude, ChatGPT and Codex, Cursor, Gemini, and Suno. Pictures are real screens, boxes and disks from my own archive. A few are drawn: the video labels, the phone call card, the Amiga boot diagram, the demo scroller, the Pod window, and the tracker screen (the notes are real, from the song). Code lives at github.com/hurtz.
