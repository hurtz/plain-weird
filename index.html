Plain Weird

Games, retro teardowns and weird machines, all made by Jason O'Connell

These are my games and my ideas. I've been in IT for 32 years, starting at a Phoenix computer store in 1994. Now I build at night in Des Moines with AI as my crew: remakes of the games I grew up on, teardowns of their code, a TV I can swear at, and an AI that phones businesses for me. Most of the games run right here in your browser. Hover for the action, click to play.
