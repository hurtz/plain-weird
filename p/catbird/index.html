CatBirdDSM

CatBirdDSM is a free browser game made by Jason O'Connell (hurtz2k) in Des Moines, Iowa.

A cute little catbird with big, messy plans. Eat everything in Des Moines, then poop on the parade, the governor and the police. Spring, summer, fall and snow, day or night. Multiplayer, with its own crowd on Reddit.
